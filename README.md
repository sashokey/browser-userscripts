Userscripts for desktop Ozon, Twitch recordings, desktop and mobile YouTube, and YouTube Music. Requires [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/).

## Installation

Open an installation link, confirm in Violentmonkey, and reload the target site. Enable **Allow User Scripts** for Violentmonkey if your browser requires it.

### Ozon - Rating and Review Filters

Adds rating and review-count ranges to listings and a delivery filter to Ozon Fresh. [Install Ozon - Rating and Review Filters](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sashokey/browser-userscripts/master/ozon-search/ozon-search.user.js)

### Skip Twitch Muted Segments

Automatically skips muted segments during Twitch recording playback. [Install Skip Twitch Muted Segments](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sashokey/browser-userscripts/master/skip-twitch-muted-segments/skip-twitch-muted-segments.user.js)

### YouTube Tools

Adds recommendation feedback, watched-video filtering, audio-only playback, and manual playlist advancement on YouTube. Filters music videos out of YouTube Music listings and playback. [Install YouTube Tools](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sashokey/browser-userscripts/master/youtube-tools/youtube-tools.user.js)

## Usage notes

- When moving an existing installation, disable the previous Ozon and YouTube entries before installing their new copies. Set your YouTube preferences again in the new copy. Reopen the Twitch installation link to move it to the new update source.
- Ozon filters apply to loaded cards and reset when the search or category changes. Ozon determines final delivery options in the cart.
- YouTube exposes **Watched videos** and **Audio only** in the desktop account menu and mobile **Settings**. Audio-only changes apply on the next video load. In playlists, use YouTube's **Next**, **Previous**, or a playlist item to continue.
